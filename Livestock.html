LIVESTOCK PRODUCTION

Welcome to the Livestock Production Section of the Egerton University Department. We are dedicated to advancing animal agriculture through hands-on education, innovative research, and sustainable management practices. Our focus spans across modern dairy management, beef cattle husbandry, and efficient poultry farming techniques. By intergrating modern breeding techniques, optimized animal nuitrition, and disease management strategies, our department prepares students and local farm enteprise to meet growingfood demands while prioritizing animal welfare and environmental sustainability.
